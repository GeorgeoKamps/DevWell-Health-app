# Hydration at the Desk

It's easy to go hours without water when you're deep in a problem, but even mild dehydration hurts concentration and causes headaches.

## How much
A common target is around 2-2.5 litres of water a day, more if you drink coffee (which is mildly dehydrating) or exercise. Thirst is a late signal — don't wait for it.

## Make it automatic
Keep a large water bottle on your desk in view. Refilling it becomes a natural standing break. Pair drinking with a routine, like every time you push code or finish a meeting.

## Watch the coffee
Coffee counts toward fluids but the caffeine is a mild diuretic and can spike then crash your energy. Alternate cups of coffee with glasses of water.
