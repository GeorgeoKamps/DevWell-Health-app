# Ergonomic Desk Setup

Poor setup causes neck, wrist, and back pain over months of coding.

## Monitor
Top of the screen at or slightly below eye level, about an arm's length away. This keeps your neck neutral instead of craned down.

## Chair and posture
Feet flat on the floor, knees roughly level with hips, lower back supported. Sit back in the chair rather than perching forward.

## Keyboard and mouse
Elbows at about 90 degrees, wrists straight and floating (not resting bent on the desk). A wrist rest helps only when typing pauses.

## The 20-20-20 rule for eyes
Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet (about 6 metres) away for 20 seconds. This relaxes the eye muscles and reduces digital eye strain.

## Move regularly
Even a perfect setup is harmful if static. Stand or walk for a couple of minutes every 30-45 minutes.
