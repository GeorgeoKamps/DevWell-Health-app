# Eye Care and Screen Strain

Staring at code all day causes digital eye strain: dryness, blurred vision, and headaches.

## The 20-20-20 rule
Every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet (6 metres) away for 20 seconds. This relaxes the focusing muscles that lock up when you stare at a close screen.

## Blink on purpose
People blink up to 60% less when looking at screens, which dries the eyes. Consciously blink fully and often, and consider lubricating eye drops if your eyes feel gritty.

## Adjust brightness and contrast
Match your screen brightness to the room — a screen much brighter than its surroundings strains the eyes. Increase font size rather than leaning in.

## Position and lighting
Keep the screen about an arm's length away with the top at or just below eye level. Avoid glare from windows or overhead lights hitting the screen.
