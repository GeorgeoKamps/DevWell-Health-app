# Sleep for Developers

Irregular hours and late-night coding wreck sleep, which then wrecks focus the next day.

## Keep a consistent schedule
Going to bed and waking at the same time — even on weekends — matters more than total hours. It stabilizes your circadian rhythm so you fall asleep faster.

## Cut screens before bed
Avoid bright screens 30-60 minutes before sleep. Blue light suppresses melatonin, the hormone that signals your body to wind down. If you must work late, use night mode and dim the room.

## Wind-down routine
A short routine tells your brain it's time to switch off: dim lights, no more Slack, a few minutes of stretching or reading on paper.

## Caffeine cutoff
Caffeine has a half-life of ~5-6 hours, so an afternoon coffee can still be in your system at bedtime. Try to stop caffeine by early afternoon.

## If you can't sleep
Don't lie in bed frustrated. Get up, do something calm and dim for 15 minutes, then try again. Associating the bed with wakefulness makes insomnia worse.
