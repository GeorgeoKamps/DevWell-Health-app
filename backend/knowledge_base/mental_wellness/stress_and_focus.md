# Managing Stress and Focus While Coding

## When you're stuck and frustrated
Step away for 5 minutes. Stepping away from a bug often surfaces the solution because your brain keeps working in the background (the "incubation effect").

## Box breathing for acute stress
Inhale 4 seconds, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4. Repeat 4 cycles. It calms the nervous system in under two minutes.

## Pomodoro for focus
Work in 25-minute focused blocks with 5-minute breaks. After four blocks, take a longer 15-30 minute break. Protects against burnout.

## Beating the afternoon slump
Hydrate, get natural light, and take a short walk rather than reaching for more caffeine, which can worsen the later crash.

## Protecting sleep as a developer
Avoid screens 30-60 minutes before bed; blue light suppresses melatonin. Consistent sleep and wake times matter more than total hours.

If stress feels persistent or overwhelming rather than situational, talking to a mental-health professional is a sign of strength, not weakness.
