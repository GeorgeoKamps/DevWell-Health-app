# Energy, Caffeine, and the Afternoon Slump

That 3pm wall is normal — it's a natural dip in your circadian rhythm. How you handle it matters.

## Don't just add caffeine
A late coffee fixes the slump short-term but can wreck your sleep, which deepens tomorrow's slump. If you need caffeine, keep it to the morning and early afternoon.

## Better resets
- A 5-10 minute walk, ideally outside in daylight
- A glass of water (slumps are often mild dehydration)
- A protein-and-fiber snack instead of sugar
- 2 minutes of stretching or stair climbing to get blood moving

## Protect your peak hours
Most people focus best in the late morning. Schedule your hardest coding then, and save low-focus tasks (email, reviews) for the slump.
