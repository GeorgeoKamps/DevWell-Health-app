# Healthy Desk Snacks

The snack drawer makes or breaks an afternoon. These are quick, protein- or fiber-rich, and beat the vending machine.

## Savory
- Greek yogurt with a sprinkle of seeds
- A handful of almonds or walnuts
- Hummus with carrot and cucumber sticks
- Edamame (microwave from frozen in 3 minutes)
- Wholegrain crackers with cheese

## Sweet
- Apple or banana with a spoon of peanut butter
- A square of dark chocolate with a few berries
- Cottage cheese with pineapple

## Why these
They combine protein or fiber with slow carbs, so your energy stays level instead of spiking and crashing like it does after candy or pastries.
