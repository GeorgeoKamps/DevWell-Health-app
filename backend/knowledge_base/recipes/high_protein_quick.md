# High-Protein Meals Under 20 Minutes

Developers often skip protein when busy. These meals are fast and keep you full through long coding sessions.

## Chickpea & quinoa power bowl
Cook 1 cup quinoa (15 min). Toss with a drained can of chickpeas, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, olive oil, and lemon. ~25g protein, ~20 minutes, vegetarian.

## Greek yogurt protein bowl
1 cup plain Greek yogurt, a handful of granola, berries, and a spoon of peanut butter. ~25g protein, under 5 minutes. Great breakfast or snack.

## Egg & black bean scramble
Scramble 3 eggs with half a can of black beans, spinach, and salsa. ~28g protein, ~10 minutes.

## Tuna avocado wrap
Mash a can of tuna with half an avocado, spread on a wholegrain wrap, add greens. ~30g protein, ~7 minutes.

Tip: keep canned beans, eggs, Greek yogurt, and frozen veg on hand so a high-protein meal is always 10 minutes away.
