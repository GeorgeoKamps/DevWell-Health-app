# Easy Vegetarian Dinners

## Stir-fry tofu and vegetables
Press and cube firm tofu, pan-fry until golden, add mixed vegetables and soy-ginger sauce. Serve over rice. ~20 minutes.

## Lentil curry
Simmer red lentils with onion, garlic, curry paste, and coconut milk for 20 minutes. High in fiber and plant protein.

## Veggie pasta primavera
Saute seasonal vegetables, toss with wholewheat pasta, olive oil, garlic, and parmesan. Quick and filling.

Batch-cooking lentils or grains on Sunday makes weeknight vegetarian dinners a 10-minute assembly job.
