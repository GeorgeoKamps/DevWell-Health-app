# Quick Bodyweight Workouts

No gym needed. These fit between standups.

## 10-minute energizer
30s jumping jacks, 30s bodyweight squats, 30s push-ups, 30s plank. Repeat the circuit 3 times. Raises your heart rate and refocuses your mind.

## Lower-back relief routine
Cat-cow stretch x10, child's pose 30s, glute bridges x15, bird-dog x10 each side. Targets the back pain that comes from prolonged sitting.

## Posture reset (5 min)
Wall angels x10, doorway chest stretch 30s each side, chin tucks x10. Undoes hunching over the keyboard.

For back pain specifically, prioritize gentle mobility (cat-cow, glute bridges) over high-impact moves.
