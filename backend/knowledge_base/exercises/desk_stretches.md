# Desk Stretches for Developers

Sitting tightens hips, shoulders, and the neck. These take 5 minutes and need no equipment.

## Neck release
Slowly tilt your right ear toward your right shoulder, hold 15-20 seconds, switch sides. Relieves tension from looking down at screens.

## Shoulder rolls
Roll your shoulders backward 10 times, then forward 10 times. Counters the rounded-forward posture of typing.

## Seated spinal twist
Sit tall, place your right hand on the back of your chair, and gently twist to the right. Hold 20 seconds each side.

## Hip flexor stretch
Stand, step one foot back into a lunge, and tuck your pelvis. Hip flexors shorten from prolonged sitting; this lengthens them.

## Wrist and forearm stretch
Extend one arm, pull the fingers back gently with the other hand to stretch the forearm. Important for preventing typing strain.
